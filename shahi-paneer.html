North Indian 45 mins 4 servings Vegetarian

Shahi Paneer

Shahi paneer is mild, slightly sweet, and creamy. A smooth cashew base and gentle spices keep it festive without heavy heat.

Ingredients

  • 250 g paneer, cubed
  • 2 onions, roughly chopped
  • 10–12 cashews, soaked 15 minutes
  • 1 tomato, roughly chopped (optional, for slight tang)
  • 1 tablespoon ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 tablespoons ghee or oil
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 2 green cardamom
  • ½ teaspoon garam masala
  • ½ teaspoon Kashmiri chilli powder
  • 2–3 tablespoons cream
  • Salt to taste
  • Pinch of sugar if needed
  • Warm water as needed

Method

  1. Soak cashews in warm water so they blend silky-smooth. Soften fridge-hard paneer in warm water for 10 minutes if needed, then drain.
  2. Heat a little ghee; add bay leaf and cardamom. Sauté onion until soft and lightly golden. Add tomato if using and cook until soft. Cool slightly.
  3. Blend the onion mixture with drained cashews into a very smooth paste. Add a splash of water if the blender needs help.
  4. In the same pan, heat remaining ghee. Add ginger-garlic paste and stir 30 seconds. Pour in the blended paste. Cook on medium heat, stirring, until the mixture thickens and the fat begins to separate at the edges.
  5. Add Kashmiri chilli, salt, and a little warm water to make a flowing gravy. Simmer 5 minutes. Add paneer and cook gently 3–4 minutes only.
  6. Finish with cream, garam masala, and a pinch of sugar if the gravy tastes sharp. Rest 2 minutes and serve with naan or pulao.

Tips for best results

  • Blend until completely smooth for a restaurant texture.
  • Add paneer late so it stays soft.
  • Kashmiri chilli colours the gravy without making it very spicy.
