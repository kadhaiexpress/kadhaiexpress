About Kadhai Express

Kadhai Express is a free public resource dedicated to vegetarian food — recipes, techniques, ingredients, and the stories behind dishes across India and the world.

Our focus is practical home cooking and clear knowledge: how flavours are built, how regional traditions differ, and how plant-based meals can be satisfying every day.

What you’ll find

  • Full vegetarian recipes with ingredients and steps
  • Articles on food history, regions, and ingredients
  • Technique guides for everyday cooking

Editorial approach

We write for accuracy and clarity. Recipes are tested in spirit for home kitchens. We do not promote extreme health claims. Feedback and corrections are welcome via the Contact page.

Support

The site may be supported by advertising so content can stay free. Thank you for reading and cooking with us.
