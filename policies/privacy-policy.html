Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 29, 2026

Kadhai Express (“we”, “our”, or “us”) operates this website to share vegetarian recipes, food history, and culinary knowledge. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

1. Information we collect

Information you provide: If you email us, we receive your address and message content.

Automatically collected: Browser type, device type, approximate location (country/city), pages visited, and referring URLs via logs or analytics.

Cookies: We use cookies for essential functions, preferences, analytics, and advertising through Google AdSense and similar partners. You can control cookies in your browser settings.

2. How we use information

  • To operate and improve the website
  • To respond to inquiries
  • To understand traffic with analytics
  • To display relevant advertising through Google AdSense and similar partners
  • To protect the security of the site

3. Advertising

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to this or other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits.

You may opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings and learn more at aboutads.info.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share data with service providers (hosting, analytics, advertising, email) or when required by law.

5. Children’s privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Contact us to request deletion if needed.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may request access, correction, or deletion of personal data we hold. Email us to make a request.

7. Contact

Privacy questions: contact@kadhaiexpress.com (or the address listed on our Contact page).
