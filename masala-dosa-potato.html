Masala Dosa Potato Filling (Aloo Masala)

Aloo masala for dosa should be soft, lightly tangy, and aromatic from mustard and curry leaves — chunky enough to sit inside a crisp dosa without turning pasty.

Ingredients

  • 4 medium potatoes, boiled, peeled, and roughly crushed
  • 1 onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 teaspoon mustard seeds
  • 1 teaspoon urad dal
  • 1 teaspoon chana dal
  • 10–12 curry leaves
  • 1–2 green chillies, slit
  • ½ teaspoon turmeric
  • 1 tablespoon oil
  • Salt to taste
  • Optional: grated ginger, lemon juice, chopped coriander
  • Pinch of hing (optional)

Method

  1. Boil potatoes until just tender, not waterlogged. Peel and crush roughly with a masher or hands so you still see small chunks. Fully mashed potato becomes gluey inside the dosa.
  2. Heat oil in a pan on medium heat. Add mustard seeds and wait until they pop. Add urad dal and chana dal; fry until golden. Add curry leaves, green chilli, optional ginger, and hing. Sauté a few seconds.
  3. Add the sliced onion and cook until soft and translucent, not heavily browned.
  4. Add turmeric and the crushed potatoes. Sprinkle salt. Mix gently so spices coat the potato without turning it into a paste. If it looks dry, sprinkle 1–2 tablespoons water and cover for a minute on low heat.
  5. Finish with lemon and coriander if using. Cool slightly before stuffing dosas, or serve as a side with plain dosa, roti, or poori.

Tips for best results

  • Slightly chunky potatoes taste better than a smooth mash.
  • Do not skip the dals in the temper — they add crunch and aroma.
  • Make the filling a little under-salty if your dosa batter is already salted.
