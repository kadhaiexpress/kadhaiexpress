North Indian 40 mins 4 servings Vegetarian

Kadai Paneer

Kadai paneer is defined by a coarse coriander-chilli masala and slightly crisp capsicum. Freshly crushed spices beat ready powder for this dish.

Ingredients

  • 250 g paneer, cubed
  • 1 large capsicum, cubed
  • 2 tomatoes, finely chopped or pureed
  • 1 onion, sliced or cubed
  • 1 tablespoon ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tablespoon coriander seeds
  • 2 dried red chillies
  • 1 teaspoon kasuri methi
  • ½ teaspoon turmeric
  • Salt to taste
  • 2 tablespoons oil
  • Fresh coriander

Method

  1. Dry-roast coriander seeds and dried red chillies on low heat until fragrant, 2–3 minutes. Cool and crush coarsely with a mortar or pulse briefly in a grinder — keep it rough, not a fine powder. This is the kadai masala.
  2. Heat oil in a kadhai. Lightly sauté paneer cubes until pale gold if you like firmer edges; set aside. In the same oil, sauté capsicum on medium-high heat for 2–3 minutes so it stays slightly crisp; set aside.
  3. Add onion to the pan and cook until soft. Add ginger-garlic paste; stir until raw smell goes. Add tomato and turmeric; cook until soft and the oil starts to separate.
  4. Add the coarse kadai masala and salt. Stir 30–40 seconds. Return capsicum and paneer. Toss on medium heat 3–4 minutes so everything is coated. Sprinkle a splash of water only if the masala sticks.
  5. Crush kasuri methi between your palms, add it, and mix. Garnish with coriander. Serve with roti or naan.

Tips for best results

  • Coarse masala is the signature — do not powder it too fine.
  • Keep capsicum slightly crisp.
  • Kasuri methi at the end lifts the aroma.
