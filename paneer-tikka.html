Paneer Tikka (Homemade Oven or Tawa)

Paneer tikka at home works when the marinade is thick, the paneer is patted dry, and high heat creates colour without drying the cubes out.

Ingredients

  • 250 g paneer, cut into 1-inch cubes
  • ½ cup thick hung yogurt or Greek-style yogurt
  • 1 tablespoon ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 teaspoon Kashmiri red chilli powder
  • ½ teaspoon turmeric
  • 1 teaspoon garam masala
  • 1 teaspoon chaat masala
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • 1 tablespoon oil, plus more for brushing
  • Salt to taste
  • 1 capsicum, cut into chunks
  • 1 onion, cut into chunks

Method

  1. Pat the paneer cubes dry with a clean cloth. Excess moisture stops the marinade from sticking and causes steaming instead of colour.
  2. In a bowl, whisk yogurt with ginger-garlic paste, Kashmiri chilli, turmeric, garam masala, chaat masala, lemon juice, oil, and salt until smooth and thick. The marinade should coat a spoon, not run off like water.
  3. Add paneer, capsicum, and onion. Fold gently so cubes do not break. Cover and rest at least 20–30 minutes (or up to a few hours in the fridge).
  4. For oven: preheat to 220°C / 425°F. Thread paneer and vegetables on skewers or spread on a lined tray. Brush lightly with oil. Bake 12–18 minutes, turning once, until edges colour. Optional: grill/broil 1–2 minutes for char.
  5. For tawa: heat a well-oiled heavy pan on medium-high. Place pieces without crowding. Cook, turning sides, until golden spots appear. Brush with a little oil or melted butter while cooking.
  6. Finish with a pinch of chaat masala and lemon. Serve hot with mint-coriander chutney and onion rings.

Tips for best results

  • Kashmiri chilli gives colour without harsh heat.
  • Thick yogurt is essential — watery curd slides off.
  • Do not overbake or paneer turns rubbery.
