Vegetable Korma

Vegetable korma is a gentle, slightly sweet gravy curry. Cook vegetables only until tender and simmer them briefly in a smooth nut or coconut paste so they stay distinct.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups mixed vegetables (carrot, beans, peas, potato, cauliflower)
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 tablespoon ginger-garlic paste
  • 10–12 cashews soaked, or ½ cup grated coconut
  • ½ teaspoon turmeric
  • 1 teaspoon coriander powder
  • ½ teaspoon garam masala
  • 2 tablespoons oil
  • Salt to taste
  • Optional: whole spices (bay, cloves), cream, coriander to finish

Method

  1. Cut vegetables into even pieces. Boil or steam until just tender — they will cook a little more in the gravy, so avoid making them mushy now. Drain and set aside.
  2. Heat a little oil; sauté onion until soft and light golden. Add ginger-garlic paste; cook until fragrant. Cool slightly.
  3. Blend onion mixture with soaked cashews (or coconut) into a smooth paste. Add water as needed for blending.
  4. In a pan, heat remaining oil. Optional: crackle whole spices. Add the paste and cook on medium heat, stirring, until it thickens and the raw smell goes. Add turmeric, coriander powder, and salt; cook 30 seconds.
  5. Add the cooked vegetables and enough water to make a medium gravy. Simmer 5–7 minutes so the vegetables absorb flavour. Finish with garam masala and optional cream. Garnish with coriander.
  6. Serve with roti, paratha, or soft pulao.

Tips for best results

  • Cashew paste leans North-style; coconut leans South-style.
  • Do not overcook vegetables before they enter the gravy.
  • A tiny pinch of sugar can balance sharp tomatoes if you add any.
