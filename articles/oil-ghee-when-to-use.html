Oil vs Ghee in Indian Vegetarian Cooking

Ghee adds nutty aroma; neutral oils handle high heat. Choosing wisely changes tadka and dessert outcomes.

Ghee

Ideal for dal tadka finish, sweets like halwa, and finishing rotis.

Oil

Better for deep-frying and high-heat stir-fries. Mustard oil is regional and pungent.

Balance

Many home cooks use oil to cook and a spoon of ghee to finish.
