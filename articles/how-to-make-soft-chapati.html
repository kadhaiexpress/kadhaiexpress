How to Make Soft Chapati (Roti) Every Time

Soft chapati depends more on dough handling and heat than on secret ingredients. This guide focuses on technique any home cook can repeat.

The dough

Use whole wheat atta. Add water gradually to form a soft, pliable dough — slightly softer than you think. Knead 3–5 minutes until smooth. A teaspoon of oil is optional. Rest covered at least 15–20 minutes so gluten relaxes.

Rolling

Divide into even balls. Dust lightly with dry flour — too much makes dry rotis. Roll from the centre outward, rotating, aiming for even thickness. Thick edges cook unevenly.

Cooking

Heat the tawa until properly hot. Place chapati; wait for small bubbles. Flip. When the second side has light brown spots, finish on direct flame for a puff, or press gently on the tawa. Do not overcook or it turns crisp and hard.

Storage

Stack and wrap in a cloth. Softness fades if left open to air. For later, cool fully before sealing to avoid sogginess.
