Rice Types for Indian Vegetarian Cooking

Different dishes need different rice: long-grain for biryani, medium for everyday meals, short parboiled styles for some regional plates.

Basmati

Best for biryani, pulao, and jeera rice when aged and fragrant.

Sona masoori / regional

Everyday rice and lemon rice; softer bite.

Idli rice

Parboiled short grains help fermentation and soft idli texture.
