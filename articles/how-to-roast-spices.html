How to Dry-Roast Spices

Dry-roasting wakes up essential oils in cumin, coriander, and dried chillies before grinding or tempering.

Method

Use a heavy pan on medium-low. Stir until fragrant and a shade darker — not black.

Cool

Cool fully before grinding or the spice cake turns pasty.

Batch

Roast small amounts you will use soon; ground spices fade faster.
