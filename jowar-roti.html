Jowar Roti (Sorghum Bhakri)

Jowar roti (bhakri) needs warm water and confident patting. The goal is an even medium-thick disc that cooks soft without dry cracks.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups jowar (sorghum) flour
  • Warm water as needed
  • Salt to taste
  • Optional ghee to finish

Method

  1. Place jowar flour in a bowl with salt. Add warm water gradually and knead a soft dough. Cover and rest 5 minutes.
  2. Take a portion and knead again briefly. Pat on a flat surface into an even round. Wet hands help seal edge cracks.
  3. Cook on a medium-hot tawa. When the underside firms, flip. Press lightly with a cloth for even cooking. Optional direct flame for puff.
  4. Finish with ghee if desired. Serve with pithla, thecha, or vegetable curry.

Tips for best results

  • Soft dough cracks less than stiff dough.
  • Press gently while cooking to avoid hard spots.
