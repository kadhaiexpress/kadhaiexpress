Palak Paneer

Palak paneer should taste of fresh spinach, not bitterness or raw spice. Blanching, a smooth puree, and a short simmer with paneer keep the colour and texture right.

Ingredients

  • 250 g paneer, cubed
  • 500 g spinach, washed well
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 tomato, chopped
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 green chillies
  • ½ tsp cumin seeds
  • ½ tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • ½ tsp garam masala
  • 2 tbsp oil or ghee
  • Salt
  • 2 tbsp cream or cashew paste (optional)

Method

  1. Bring a pot of water to a boil. Add spinach leaves and blanch for only 2 minutes — long boiling dulls the green colour. Immediately transfer to a bowl of ice-cold water. Drain and blend with green chillies to a smooth puree.
  2. Heat oil in a kadhai. Add cumin seeds. When they crackle, add onion and cook until golden. Add ginger-garlic paste; stir until the raw smell fades. Add tomato and cook until soft and mushy.
  3. Add turmeric, coriander powder, and salt. Stir in the spinach puree. Add a splash of water if it is too thick. Simmer 5–7 minutes on medium-low so the puree cooks through but stays green.
  4. Add paneer cubes and cook 3–4 minutes gently. Finish with garam masala and optional cream or cashew paste. Do not boil hard after adding cream.
  5. Serve with roti, naan, or jeera rice.

Tips for best results

  • Ice bath after blanching locks colour.
  • Lightly pan-sear paneer first if you like firmer edges.
  • Cashew paste gives body without dairy cream.
