Gujarati 45 mins 4 servings Vegetarian

Khaman Dhokla

Soft khaman dhokla depends on a pourable besan batter, Eno added at the last moment, and steady steaming without opening the lid early.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups besan (gram flour)
  • 1 tablespoon semolina/rava (optional, for texture)
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • 1 teaspoon lemon juice or citric acid
  • About 1 cup water (adjust)
  • 1 teaspoon Eno fruit salt (or fruit salt), added last
  • ¼ teaspoon turmeric
  • Salt to taste
  • For tempering: 2 tablespoons oil, 1 teaspoon mustard seeds, 2 green chillies slit, curry leaves, 1 teaspoon sesame seeds optional
  • Coriander and grated coconut, to garnish

Method

  1. Sieve besan into a bowl. Add rava if using, turmeric, salt, sugar, and lemon juice. Whisk in water gradually to a smooth, pourable batter — thicker than dosa batter but not stiff. Rest 10 minutes.
  2. Prepare a steamer: bring water to a boil and grease a dhokla plate or thali. When ready to steam, add Eno to the batter and stir in one direction until the batter turns frothy and light. Do not wait long after adding Eno.
  3. Pour the batter into the greased plate immediately. Steam on medium heat for 15–18 minutes until a toothpick comes out clean and the top springs back. Do not open the steamer in the first 12 minutes.
  4. Cool 5 minutes, then cut into squares.
  5. Heat oil for tempering. Add mustard seeds; when they pop, add chilli, curry leaves, and sesame. Add 2–3 tablespoons water carefully (it will sputter) and a pinch of sugar if you like a mild sweet glaze; pour over the pieces so it seeps into the cuts.
  6. Garnish with coriander and coconut. Serve with green chutney.

Tips for best results

  • Eno must go in just before steaming.
  • Batter consistency controls softness more than extra Eno.
  • Over-steaming dries the dhokla.
