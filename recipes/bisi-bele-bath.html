Bisi Bele Bath

Bisi bele bath means hot lentil-rice dish: rice and toor dal cooked with vegetables and a distinctive spice mix, finished with ghee and tempered spices. A complete Karnataka vegetarian meal in one pot.

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup rice
  • 1/2 cup toor dal
  • 2 cups mixed vegetables (carrot, beans, peas, potato)
  • 2–3 tbsp bisi bele bath powder
  • Tamarind small piece or 1 tsp paste
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • Salt
  • Ghee
  • Temper: mustard, peanuts, curry leaves, dried red chilli

Method

  1. Cook rice and dal together with turmeric until soft (slightly mashable).
  2. Cook vegetables separately or in the same pot with enough water.
  3. Add tamarind water, bisi bele bath powder, salt; simmer so flavours blend. Consistency should be soft and moist, not dry like pulao.
  4. Temper spices and peanuts in ghee; pour over. Serve hot with boondi or potato chips and salad.

Tips

  • Authentic powder makes a large difference — use a good brand or homemade mix.
  • Ghee is traditional and aromatic.
  • Adjust thickness with hot water before serving.
