Curd Rice (Thayir Sadam)

Curd rice is cooling, gentle, and deeply satisfying: mashed rice mixed with yogurt and a fragrant tempering. A South Indian vegetarian staple for everyday meals and travel.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked rice, slightly mashable
  • 1–1.5 cups fresh yogurt
  • Milk splash if needed
  • Salt
  • Temper: 1 tsp oil, mustard, urad dal, ginger, green chilli, curry leaves, hing
  • Optional: pomegranate, coriander, grated carrot

Method

  1. Mash rice lightly while still warm. Cool a bit.
  2. Mix yogurt and salt; add milk if too thick. Combine with rice.
  3. Temper spices in oil; pour over rice mixture; fold in.
  4. Garnish as desired. Serve at room temperature with pickle.

Tips

  • Slightly overcooked rice mixes better.
  • Use fresh yogurt — very sour curd can dominate.
  • Tempering is the flavour — do not skip ginger and curry leaves.
