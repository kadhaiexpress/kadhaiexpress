Paneer Tikka (Homemade Oven or Tawa)

Paneer tikka is marinated paneer charred with peppers and onion. At home, a hot oven or tawa delivers excellent results without a tandoor.

Ingredients

  • 250 g paneer, cubes
  • 1/2 cup thick yogurt
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tsp Kashmiri chilli powder
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp chaat masala
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Salt
  • Capsicum and onion chunks

Method

  1. Mix marinade ingredients; coat paneer and vegetables. Rest 20–30 minutes (or longer refrigerated).
  2. Thread on skewers or spread on a tray. Brush oil.
  3. Bake at 220°C / 425°F until edges colour (12–18 min), or cook on a well-oiled tawa turning sides.
  4. Finish with chaat masala and lemon. Serve with mint chutney.

Tips

  • Pat paneer dry so marinade sticks.
  • Kashmiri chilli gives colour without extreme heat.
  • A brief broil/grill at the end adds char.
