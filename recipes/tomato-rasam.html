Tomato Rasam

Rasam is a light, soupy South Indian preparation — pepper, cumin, tomato, and tamarind in a thin broth that aids digestion and complements rice or stands alone in a cup.

Ingredients

  • 3 ripe tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 tsp tamarind paste (or small marble of tamarind)
  • 1 tsp rasam powder (or crushed pepper + cumin)
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • 1 dried red chilli
  • Mustard seeds, curry leaves, hing
  • 1 tbsp oil or ghee
  • Salt, coriander
  • 2 cups water

Method

  1. Cook tomatoes with water, turmeric, salt, and tamarind until soft; mash lightly.
  2. Add rasam powder; simmer 5 minutes. Do not overboil after powder if using aromatic mix.
  3. Temper mustard, chilli, curry leaves, hing in oil/ghee; pour over rasam.
  4. Garnish with coriander. Serve with rice and appalam, or as a clear soup.

Tips

  • Freshly crushed pepper-cumin tastes brighter than old powder.
  • Keep rasam thin — it is not a thick dal.
  • A crushed garlic clove in the temper is common in some homes.
