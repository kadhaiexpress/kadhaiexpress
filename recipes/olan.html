Olan (Ash Gourd & Cowpeas)

Olan is a mild Kerala stew of ash gourd and cowpeas in thin coconut milk — subtle and vegetarian.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups ash gourd cubes
  • 1/2 cup black-eyed peas, cooked
  • 1 cup thin coconut milk
  • 1/2 cup thick coconut milk
  • Green chilli
  • Curry leaves
  • Coconut oil
  • Salt

Method

  1. Cook ash gourd with chilli and salt.
  2. Add peas and thin coconut milk; simmer.
  3. Add thick milk; warm gently without boiling.
  4. Finish with coconut oil and curry leaves.

Tips

  • Boiling thick coconut milk can split it.
  • Keep flavours mild.
