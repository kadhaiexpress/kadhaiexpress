Undhiyu-Style Mixed Winter Vegetables

This home-style undhiyu-inspired mix uses seasonal vegetables, mild spices, and a coconut-coriander paste without requiring every traditional component.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups mixed vegetables (surti papdi, potato, sweet potato, brinjal, banana optional)
  • 1 cup methi muthiya (optional ready or homemade)
  • 1 cup grated coconut
  • 1 cup coriander
  • 2 green chillies
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic
  • 1 tsp ajwain
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 2 tsp coriander powder
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • Salt
  • Sugar pinch

Method

  1. Blend coconut, coriander, chilli, ginger-garlic with a little water to a coarse paste.
  2. Heat oil; ajwain. Add harder vegetables first with turmeric and salt; sauté.
  3. Add paste and coriander powder; mix. Add water; cover and cook until vegetables are tender.
  4. Add muthiya if using; simmer 5 minutes. Balance with sugar pinch. Serve with puri.

Tips

  • Cook denser vegetables longer than soft ones.
  • Fresh coconut paste is the soul of the dish.
