Tomato Bath

Tomato bath cooks rice with a tomato masala base for a one-pot vegetarian meal.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 cups rice
  • 3 tomatoes
  • 1 onion
  • Ginger-garlic
  • Biryani leaves, cloves
  • Chilli powder
  • Oil
  • Salt

Method

  1. Sauté onion and whole spices.
  2. Add tomato and masala.
  3. Add rice and water; cook until done.

Tips

  • Ripe tomatoes give better body.
