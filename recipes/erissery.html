Erissery (Pumpkin & Cowpeas)

Erissery combines pumpkin and cowpeas with coconut — Onam sadya classic.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups pumpkin
  • 1/2 cup cowpeas cooked
  • Coconut
  • Cumin
  • Green chilli
  • Mustard
  • Curry leaves
  • Coconut oil

Method

  1. Cook pumpkin.
  2. Add cowpeas and ground coconut.
  3. Temper with coconut oil.

Tips

  • Slight mash of pumpkin thickens naturally.
