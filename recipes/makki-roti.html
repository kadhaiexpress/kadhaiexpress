Makki di Roti (Maize Flatbread)

Makki roti is thicker than wheat chapati. Warm water and patient tawa cooking keep the maize flatbread soft enough to fold with saag.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups makki atta (maize flour)
  • Warm water as needed
  • Salt to taste
  • Ghee, to finish

Method

  1. Mix makki atta and salt. Add warm water little by little and knead a soft dough. Cover and rest 10 minutes.
  2. Divide into balls. Pat into medium-thick rounds — rolling thin causes cracks. Use parchment or a wet cloth if sticking.
  3. Cook on a medium tawa until brown spots appear on both sides. Apply ghee and press gently for even colour.
  4. Keep stacked and covered. Serve hot with sarson-style greens, white butter, and jaggery.

Tips for best results

  • Thicker rotis cook more evenly than paper-thin ones.
  • Ghee at the end softens the crumb.
