Tomato Dal

Andhra-style tangy tomato dal.

Ingredients

  • Toor dal
  • Tomatoes
  • Green chilli
  • Mustard temper
  • Salt

Method

  1. Cook dal with tomato.
  2. Temper generously.

Tips

  • Slightly thinner than North dal styles.
