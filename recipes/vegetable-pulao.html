Vegetable Pulao

Vegetable pulao is a complete one-pot meal: rice, vegetables, and whole spices cooked together for weeknight ease and festive thalis alike.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 cups basmati, rinsed and soaked
  • 2 cups mixed vegetables (carrot, beans, peas, potato)
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 cups water (adjust)
  • 2 tbsp oil or ghee
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 2 cloves, 1 bay leaf, 1 small cinnamon stick
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp salt
  • Coriander and lemon to serve

Method

  1. Heat oil; whole spices and cumin. Sauté onion until light golden.
  2. Add ginger-garlic; then vegetables; sauté 2–3 minutes. Add turmeric.
  3. Add drained rice; mix gently. Add water and salt.
  4. Cook covered on low until rice is done. Rest 5 minutes; fluff.
  5. Garnish and serve with raita or plain yogurt.

Tips

  • Keep vegetable pieces even so they cook with the rice.
  • Avoid stirring aggressively once water is added.
