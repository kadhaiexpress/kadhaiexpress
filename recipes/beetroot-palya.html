Beetroot Palya

Beetroot palya is sweet-earthy with coconut and tempering.

Ingredients

  • 2 beetroots
  • Mustard
  • Chana dal
  • Coconut
  • Oil
  • Salt

Method

  1. Temper; add beetroot.
  2. Cook until soft.
  3. Add coconut.

Tips

  • Wear gloves if staining is a concern.
