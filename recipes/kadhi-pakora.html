Kadhi Pakora

Kadhi pakora pairs a tangy besan-yogurt gravy with soft onion pakoras. Slow simmering develops the signature flavour of this North Indian vegetarian classic.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup sour yogurt, whisked
  • 3–4 tbsp besan (for kadhi)
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp red chilli powder
  • Salt
  • 4 cups water (approx)
  • For pakora: 1 cup besan, 1 onion sliced, chilli, salt, water, oil to fry
  • Temper: oil/ghee, mustard or cumin, dried red chilli, curry leaves, hing

Method

  1. Mix yogurt, besan, turmeric, chilli, salt smooth. Add water.
  2. Cook on medium, stirring often until the mixture boils and thickens slightly (15–20 min). Simmer gently.
  3. Make pakora batter; fry small onion pakoras until golden. Drain.
  4. Add pakoras to kadhi; simmer 5 minutes so they soften.
  5. Temper spices in ghee; pour over kadhi. Serve with rice.

Tips

  • Stir kadhi until it first boils or besan may split.
  • Sour yogurt gives authentic tang.
  • Pakoras can be made slightly soft — they firm in hot kadhi.
