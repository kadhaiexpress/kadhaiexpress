Recipes / Kadhi Pakora

North Indian 55 mins 4 servings Vegetarian

Kadhi Pakora

Kadhi pakora is a tangy besan-yogurt gravy with soft onion fritters. The kadhi must be stirred until it first boils so the besan does not split, then simmered until the raw taste is gone.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup sour yogurt (slightly sour is ideal), whisked smooth
  • 4 tablespoons besan (gram flour) for the kadhi
  • ½ teaspoon turmeric powder
  • ½–1 teaspoon red chilli powder
  • About 4 cups water
  • Salt to taste
  • For pakoras: 1 cup besan, 1 large onion thinly sliced, 1 green chilli chopped, pinch of carom seeds (ajwain), salt, water, oil for frying
  • For tempering: 1 tablespoon ghee or oil, 1 teaspoon mustard or cumin seeds, 2 dried red chillies, curry leaves, pinch of hing

Method

  1. In a large bowl, whisk yogurt and 4 tablespoons besan until completely smooth with no lumps. Add turmeric, chilli powder, salt, and water. Mix well. The mixture will look thin; it thickens as it cooks.
  2. Pour into a heavy pot and place on medium heat. Stir almost continuously until the kadhi comes to a full boil — this step prevents the besan from splitting. Once it boils, lower the heat and simmer 20–25 minutes, stirring now and then, until the raw besan smell is gone and the kadhi tastes cooked and lightly thick.
  3. Meanwhile, make the pakora batter: mix 1 cup besan with onion, chilli, ajwain, salt, and just enough water to form a thick dropping batter. Heat oil for frying. Drop small portions and fry on medium heat until golden and cooked through. Drain on paper.
  4. Slide the pakoras into the simmering kadhi and cook 5 minutes so they soften and absorb flavour. Do not boil aggressively or they may break.
  5. Heat ghee for tempering. Add mustard or cumin, dried red chillies, curry leaves, and hing. When aromatic, pour over the kadhi. Serve with steamed rice.

Tips for best results

  • Slightly sour yogurt gives authentic tang.
  • Stir until the first boil — the most important technique tip.
  • Pakoras can be slightly soft; they firm up in the hot kadhi.
