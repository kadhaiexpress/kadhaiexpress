Kanda Poha

Poha turns mushy when soaked like rice. Rinse briefly, drain well, and toss on low heat so grains stay separate and light.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups thick poha (flattened rice)
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 2 tbsp peanuts
  • 1 tsp mustard seeds
  • Curry leaves
  • 1–2 green chillies
  • ½ tsp turmeric
  • 1–2 tbsp oil
  • Salt
  • Pinch of sugar
  • Lemon juice
  • Coriander
  • Optional: boiled potato cubes, sev

Method

  1. Place thick poha in a colander. Rinse under running water for a few seconds, toss, and let all water drain. Spread in a bowl and sprinkle salt, turmeric, and a pinch of sugar. Fluff with your fingers. The flakes should feel moist and soft, not wet or sticky. Rest 3–4 minutes.
  2. Heat oil in a wide pan. Add mustard seeds; when they pop, add peanuts and fry until golden. Add curry leaves, green chilli, and onion. Sauté until onion turns translucent.
  3. Add optional potato. Lower the heat. Add the seasoned poha and toss gently for 2–3 minutes until hot and evenly coloured. Do not cover for long or it steams soft.
  4. Switch off the heat. Add lemon juice and coriander. Toss once. Top with sev if using and serve immediately.

Tips for best results

  • Use thick poha; thin poha collapses easily.
  • Never soak poha in a bowl of water.
  • Assemble lemon and sev at the end.
