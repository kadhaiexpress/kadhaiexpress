Kanda Poha (Flattened Rice Breakfast)

Poha is a quick vegetarian breakfast: flattened rice brightened with onion, turmeric, peanuts, and lemon. Light, filling, and ready in minutes.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups thick poha
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 tbsp peanuts
  • 1 tsp mustard seeds
  • Curry leaves, green chilli
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • 1–2 tbsp oil
  • Salt, sugar pinch
  • Lemon, coriander, optional sev

Method

  1. Rinse poha in a colander; drain well. Sprinkle salt, turmeric, sugar; fluff. It should be moist, not soggy.
  2. Heat oil; mustard; peanuts until golden. Add chilli, curry leaves, onion; sauté until soft.
  3. Add poha; toss gently on low heat 2–3 minutes.
  4. Finish with lemon and coriander. Optional sev on top.

Tips

  • Thick poha holds texture better than thin.
  • Do not soak poha in a bowl of water — it turns mushy.
  • A boiled potato cube is a popular Mumbai-style addition.
