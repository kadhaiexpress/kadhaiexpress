Baingan Bharta (Smoky Mashed Eggplant)

Baingan bharta gets its character from smoky roasted eggplant mashed into a simple onion-tomato masala. A classic vegetarian dish with roti or paratha.

Ingredients

  • 1 large eggplant (baingan)
  • 2 onions, finely chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 green chillies
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Salt, coriander, lemon

Method

  1. Roast eggplant over open flame or under a grill until skin chars and flesh collapses. Cool, peel, mash.
  2. Heat oil; cumin. Sauté onion until golden. Add ginger-garlic and chilli.
  3. Add tomato; cook until soft. Add turmeric, coriander, salt.
  4. Add mashed eggplant; cook 8–10 minutes, stirring, until flavours meld and moisture reduces.
  5. Finish with coriander and lemon. Serve with roti or dal-rice.

Tips

  • Open-flame roasting gives the signature smoky taste.
  • Cook off extra water so bharta is not watery.
  • A pinch of garam masala at the end is optional.
