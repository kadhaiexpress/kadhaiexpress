Paneer Butter Masala

Paneer butter masala is a mild, creamy tomato-cashew curry. A smooth gravy, balanced sweetness, and paneer added at the end keep the texture soft and restaurant-like at home.

Ingredients

  • 250 g paneer, cut into cubes
  • 3–4 ripe tomatoes, roughly chopped
  • 1 medium onion, roughly chopped (optional, for body)
  • 12–15 cashew nuts, soaked in warm water 15 minutes
  • 1 tablespoon ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 tablespoons butter, plus 1 teaspoon oil
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 2 green cardamom pods
  • ½ teaspoon turmeric
  • 1 teaspoon Kashmiri red chilli powder (for colour)
  • 1 teaspoon coriander powder
  • ½ teaspoon garam masala
  • 1 teaspoon sugar or to taste
  • 2–3 tablespoons cream (optional)
  • 1 teaspoon kasuri methi, crushed
  • Salt to taste
  • Warm water as needed

Method

  1. Soak cashews in warm water so they blend smoothly. If your paneer is firm from the fridge, soak the cubes in warm water for 10 minutes, then drain — this softens them.
  2. In a pan, heat 1 teaspoon oil with 1 tablespoon butter. Add bay leaf and cardamom. Add onion if using and sauté until soft. Add tomatoes and a pinch of salt. Cover and cook until tomatoes collapse completely, 8–10 minutes.
  3. Cool slightly, remove whole spices if you prefer, and blend the tomato mixture with drained cashews into a very smooth puree. Add a splash of water if the blender needs help.
  4. In the same pan, heat the remaining butter. Add ginger-garlic paste and stir for 30 seconds. Pour in the blended puree. Cook on medium heat, stirring, until the gravy thickens and the raw tomato smell disappears. You should see small bubbles of fat at the edges.
  5. Add turmeric, Kashmiri chilli powder, coriander powder, and salt. Stir for a minute. Add warm water to reach a flowing but not watery consistency. Simmer 5 minutes.
  6. Add paneer cubes and simmer gently 3–4 minutes only — long boiling can make paneer rubbery. Stir in sugar, garam masala, cream if using, and crushed kasuri methi. Taste and adjust salt and sweetness.
  7. Rest 2–3 minutes off the heat. Serve with naan, roti, or jeera rice.

Tips for best results

  • Kashmiri chilli gives red colour without excessive heat.
  • Blend the gravy until completely smooth for a restaurant finish.
  • Add paneer late so it stays soft.
