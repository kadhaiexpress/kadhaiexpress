Mushroom Masala

Mushrooms in onion-tomato gravy.

Ingredients

  • Mushrooms
  • Onion
  • Tomato
  • Garam masala
  • Oil
  • Salt

Method

  1. Sauté mushrooms.
  2. Simmer in gravy.

Tips

  • Wipe mushrooms; do not soak.
