Soft Idli (Home Style)

Soft idli needs well-fermented batter and gentle steaming — the classic South Indian vegetarian breakfast.

Ingredients

  • Fermented idli batter
  • Oil for moulds
  • Optional: carrot garnish

Method

  1. Grease idli moulds. Pour batter without overfilling.
  2. Steam 10–12 minutes until a toothpick comes out clean.
  3. Rest 2 minutes; unmould. Serve with sambar and chutney.

Tips

  • Do not over-steam or idlis harden.
  • Fresh batter yields softest results.
