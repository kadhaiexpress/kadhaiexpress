Vegetable Korma

Vegetable korma is a gently spiced, slightly sweet gravy curry with mixed vegetables — popular with roti, pulao, and those who prefer milder heat.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups mixed vegetables
  • 1 onion
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic
  • 10–12 cashews (soaked) or 1/2 cup coconut
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • Whole spices optional
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Salt, coriander, optional cream

Method

  1. Boil or steam vegetables until just tender.
  2. Sauté onion and ginger-garlic. Blend with cashews/coconut to a smooth paste (add water).
  3. Cook paste in oil with powdered spices until aromatic and oil shows.
  4. Add vegetables and water/milk for gravy consistency. Simmer 5 minutes.
  5. Finish with garam masala and coriander. Serve with roti or pulao.

Tips

  • Cashew paste makes a rich North-style korma; coconut leans South.
  • Do not overcook vegetables before adding to gravy.
  • A pinch of sugar balances tomato if used.
