Mumbai style 45 mins Vegetarian

Pav Bhaji

Pav bhaji mashes spiced vegetables into a buttery street-style bhaji served with toasted pav. A full vegetarian meal with onion and lemon.

Ingredients

  • 2 potatoes
  • 1 cup cauliflower
  • 1/2 cup peas
  • 1 carrot
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 onion
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic
  • 2–3 tbsp pav bhaji masala
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • Butter or oil
  • Salt
  • Pav buns
  • Coriander, lemon, onion to serve

Method

  1. Boil vegetables until soft; mash coarsely.
  2. Sauté onion and ginger-garlic in butter; add tomato; cook soft.
  3. Add masala, turmeric, salt; then mashed vegetables. Simmer with water to a thick mash.
  4. Toast pav with butter. Serve bhaji topped with butter, onion, coriander, lemon.

Tips

  • Pav bhaji masala drives the flavour — use a blend you like.
  • Extra butter is traditional for street-style richness.
