Moong Dal Khichdi

Moong dal khichdi is gentle, digestible comfort food: rice and yellow moong cooked soft, finished with ghee-cumin tadka.

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup rice
  • 1/2 cup yellow moong dal
  • 3–3.5 cups water
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • Salt
  • 1 tbsp ghee
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • Pinch hing
  • Optional: ginger, green chilli, peas

Method

  1. Rinse rice and dal. Cook with water, turmeric, salt (and optional ginger/peas) until soft and porridge-like.
  2. Temper ghee with cumin and hing; pour over khichdi.
  3. Serve with yogurt, pickle, and papad.

Tips

  • Ratio of water controls thickness — more water for softer sick-day style.
  • Ghee tadka is essential for aroma.
