Sambar (South Indian Lentil Vegetable Stew)

Sambar is the backbone of many South Indian meals — a tangy, spiced dal-and-vegetable stew served with idli, dosa, rice, or vada. Balance of tamarind, dal, and sambar powder is the key.

Ingredients

  • 3/4 cup toor dal
  • 1 lemon-sized ball tamarind, soaked (or 1–2 tsp paste)
  • 2 cups mixed vegetables (drumstick, pumpkin, brinjal, carrot, beans)
  • 2–3 tbsp sambar powder
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • Salt to taste
  • 1 tsp mustard seeds
  • 2 dried red chillies
  • Curry leaves, hing
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Optional: small onion, tomato, jaggery pinch

Method

  1. Cook toor dal with turmeric until soft; mash lightly.
  2. Extract tamarind water. Cook vegetables in water with salt until just tender.
  3. Combine dal, vegetables, tamarind water, and sambar powder. Simmer 8–10 minutes. Adjust thickness and salt. Add jaggery if needed for balance.
  4. Temper: heat oil; mustard, red chilli, curry leaves, hing. Pour over sambar.
  5. Serve with idli, dosa, or steamed rice.

Tips for best results

  • Fresh sambar powder elevates flavour dramatically.
  • Do not overcook vegetables to mush.
  • Drumstick and pumpkin are classic; use what you have.
