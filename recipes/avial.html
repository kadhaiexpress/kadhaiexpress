Avial (Kerala Mixed Vegetables)

Avial is a thick mix of vegetables in coconut and yogurt with coconut oil and curry leaves — Onam sadya essential.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups mixed vegetables (ash gourd, carrot, beans, drumstick)
  • 1 cup grated coconut
  • 1/2 cup yogurt
  • Green chilli
  • Cumin
  • Curry leaves
  • Coconut oil
  • Salt
  • Turmeric

Method

  1. Cook vegetables with turmeric and salt until tender.
  2. Grind coconut, chilli, cumin coarsely.
  3. Add to vegetables; fold in yogurt off heat.
  4. Finish with coconut oil and curry leaves.

Tips

  • Do not boil yogurt hard.
  • Coconut oil is traditional.
