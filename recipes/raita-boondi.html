Boondi Raita

Boondi raita cools spicy meals with soft chickpea pearls in spiced yogurt.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup yogurt
  • 1/2 cup boondi
  • Cumin powder
  • Salt
  • Coriander
  • Chilli optional

Method

  1. Soak boondi briefly if hard; drain.
  2. Mix into whisked yogurt with spices.

Tips

  • Do not over-soak boondi or it turns mushy.
