Gajar Halwa (Carrot Halwa)

Gajar halwa slowly reduces grated carrots in milk with ghee and sugar until thick and fragrant. A winter vegetarian dessert that needs patience more than skill.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg red carrots, grated
  • 1 litre full-fat milk
  • 4–5 tbsp ghee
  • 3/4–1 cup sugar (to taste)
  • 4–5 cardamom pods, crushed
  • Handful cashews and raisins
  • Optional: khoya/mawa for richness

Method

  1. In a heavy kadhai, cook grated carrot with milk on medium heat, stirring often, until milk is mostly absorbed (30–45 minutes).
  2. Add ghee; continue cooking so the mixture leaves the sides.
  3. Add sugar; mixture loosens then thickens again. Cook until glossy.
  4. Add cardamom, fried nuts, and raisins. Optional khoya at the end.
  5. Serve warm or chilled.

Tips

  • Dark red Delhi carrots give the best colour.
  • Do not rush the milk reduction — flavour concentrates there.
  • Sugar amount depends on carrot sweetness.
