Aloo Gobi (Potato Cauliflower Stir-Fry)

Aloo gobi is a dry-ish sabzi where potatoes and cauliflower take on turmeric, cumin, and gentle spices without drowning in gravy. Ideal with roti or as a thali side.

Ingredients

  • 2 medium potatoes, cubed
  • 1 medium cauliflower, florets
  • 1 onion, sliced (optional)
  • 1 tomato, chopped (optional)
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • 1/2 tsp red chilli powder
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Salt, coriander leaves
  • 1 tsp ginger, grated

Method

  1. Heat oil; cumin until it crackles. Add ginger and optional onion; sauté briefly.
  2. Add potatoes; fry 3–4 minutes. Add cauliflower; turmeric, chilli, coriander, salt.
  3. Mix gently. Cover and cook on low, stirring occasionally, until vegetables are tender but not mushy (12–18 minutes). Sprinkle water only if sticking.
  4. Add tomato if using; cook off excess moisture. Finish with garam masala and coriander.
  5. Serve with roti, paratha, or dal-rice.

Tips for best results

  • Do not overcrowd the pan or florets will steam and turn soggy.
  • Slight char on edges adds flavour.
  • Keep pieces similar size for even cooking.
