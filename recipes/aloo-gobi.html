Aloo Gobi (Potato Cauliflower Stir-Fry)

Aloo gobi should be a dry-ish sabzi with tender potatoes, cauliflower that still holds shape, and spices coating each piece. Even-sized cuts and controlled water are the keys.

Ingredients

  • 2 medium potatoes, peeled and cubed
  • 1 medium cauliflower, cut into medium florets
  • 1 onion, sliced (optional)
  • 1 tomato, chopped (optional)
  • 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
  • 1 teaspoon grated ginger
  • ½ teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 teaspoon coriander powder
  • ½ teaspoon red chilli powder
  • ½ teaspoon garam masala
  • 2–3 tablespoons oil
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh coriander leaves

Method

  1. Cut potatoes and cauliflower into similar-sized pieces so they cook at a similar pace. Rinse cauliflower florets and drain well.
  2. Heat oil in a wide pan on medium heat. Add cumin seeds; when they crackle, add ginger and optional onion. Sauté briefly until fragrant.
  3. Add the potato cubes first. Stir-fry 3–4 minutes so the edges start to firm up. Then add the cauliflower, turmeric, chilli powder, coriander powder, and salt. Toss so every piece is coated.
  4. Cover and cook on low–medium heat, stirring every few minutes, until both vegetables are tender but not falling apart (about 12–18 minutes). Sprinkle only 1–2 tablespoons of water if the pan is very dry and the spices are catching. Too much water turns this into a wet curry.
  5. If using tomato, add it near the end and cook off the extra moisture. Finish with garam masala and coriander. Serve with roti, paratha, or dal-rice.

Tips for best results

  • Do not overcrowd the pan or the vegetables will steam and soften too much.
  • A light char on the edges adds flavour.
  • Keep the lid ajar toward the end if moisture collects.
