Coconut Chutney

Coconut chutney is the classic partner to idli and dosa: fresh coconut, green chilli, and a mustard-curry leaf tempering. Simple, bright, and vegetarian.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup grated fresh coconut
  • 1–2 green chillies
  • 1 small piece ginger (optional)
  • Salt
  • 2–3 tbsp roasted chana dal (optional, for body)
  • Water as needed
  • Temper: 1 tsp oil, mustard, urad dal, dried red chilli, curry leaves, hing

Method

  1. Grind coconut, chilli, ginger, salt, and optional chana dal with a little water to a slightly coarse paste.
  2. Adjust consistency with water — chutney should be spoonable, not watery.
  3. Heat oil; mustard, urad, red chilli, curry leaves, hing. Pour over chutney.
  4. Serve with idli, dosa, or vada.

Tips

  • Fresh coconut tastes best; frozen grated works if thawed.
  • Roasted chana dal thickens and mildens heat.
  • Make small batches — fresh chutney is best the same day.
