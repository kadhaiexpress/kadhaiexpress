Methi Thepla

Methi thepla are spiced fenugreek flatbreads that stay soft for travel and tiffins. A Gujarati vegetarian classic with yogurt or pickle.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups whole wheat flour
  • 1 cup fresh methi leaves, finely chopped
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp red chilli powder
  • 1 tsp coriander-cumin powder
  • 1 tbsp oil + more for cooking
  • Salt
  • Yogurt or water to knead
  • Optional: sesame seeds, grated ginger

Method

  1. Mix flour, methi, spices, salt, oil. Knead with yogurt/water to a soft dough. Rest 15 minutes.
  2. Divide; roll thin theplas.
  3. Cook on a hot tawa, applying a little oil, until brown spots appear on both sides.
  4. Stack and keep covered. Serve with yogurt, pickle, or tea.

Tips

  • Do not over-knead or theplas harden.
  • Fresh methi is brighter than dried kasuri methi (which can substitute in a pinch).
  • A spoon of oil in the dough keeps them soft longer.
