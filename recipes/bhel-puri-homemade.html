Homemade Bhel Puri

Bhel puri is assembled, not cooked: crisp puffed rice tossed with chutneys, onion, tomato, and sev for sweet-spicy-tangy crunch.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups puffed rice (murmura)
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 tomato, chopped
  • Boiled potato cubes optional
  • 1/4 cup sev
  • 2 tbsp tamarind chutney
  • 2 tbsp green chutney
  • Chaat masala, salt, lemon
  • Coriander
  • Optional: nylon sev, pomegranate

Method

  1. Lightly toast puffed rice if soft.
  2. Mix onion, tomato, potato, chutneys, chaat masala, lemon.
  3. Add puffed rice and sev; toss quickly.
  4. Serve immediately so it stays crisp.

Tips

  • Assemble only when ready to eat.
  • Balance sweet, sour, and spicy chutneys to taste.

Vegetarian. See Disclaimer.
