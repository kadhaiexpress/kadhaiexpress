Misal Pav (Vegetarian)

Misal is a spicy sprouted-bean curry topped with farsan, onion, and coriander, eaten with pav. This version stays fully vegetarian.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 cups sprouted matki (moth beans)
  • 1 onion
  • 1 tomato
  • 1 tbsp goda masala or misal masala
  • 1 tsp red chilli powder
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • Tamarind or lemon
  • Oil, mustard, cumin, garlic
  • Salt
  • Farsan, sev, onion, coriander, pav

Method

  1. Cook sprouts until tender.
  2. Make tarri: temper oil with mustard, cumin, garlic; sauté onion-tomato; add masala, chilli, turmeric, salt, water; simmer.
  3. Combine with sprouts; adjust tang with tamarind.
  4. Serve in bowls topped with farsan, onion, coriander; pav on the side.

Tips

  • Keep tarri slightly thin and spicy.
  • Add farsan only at serving.
