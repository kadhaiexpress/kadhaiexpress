Pepper Rasam (Milagu Rasam)

Milagu rasam highlights black pepper and cumin in a thin, warming broth — traditional vegetarian digestive.

Ingredients

  • 1 tsp peppercorns
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 small piece tamarind
  • Turmeric, salt
  • Garlic optional
  • Mustard, curry leaves temper
  • Coriander

Method

  1. Crush pepper-cumin. Boil tamarind water with turmeric and spice.
  2. Simmer briefly. Temper mustard and curry leaves.
  3. Serve with rice or as a clear soup.

Tips

  • Do not overboil pepper or bitterness rises.
  • Freshly crushed spices taste brighter.
