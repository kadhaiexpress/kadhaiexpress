Cabbage Thoran

Thoran is a dry coconut vegetable stir-fry. Cabbage thoran is everyday Kerala vegetarian cooking.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups shredded cabbage
  • 1/2 cup grated coconut
  • Green chilli
  • Mustard seeds
  • Curry leaves
  • Turmeric
  • Oil
  • Salt

Method

  1. Temper mustard and curry leaves.
  2. Add cabbage, turmeric, salt; stir-fry.
  3. Add coconut and chilli; cook until cabbage softens but stays bright.

Tips

  • Do not add water if possible.
  • Fresh coconut is best.
