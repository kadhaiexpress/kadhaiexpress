How to Make Dosa Batter at Home

Good dosa batter is about ratio, grind, and fermentation. This guide walks through home preparation.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups idli rice or dosa rice
  • 1 cup urad dal
  • 1/2 tsp fenugreek seeds
  • Salt after fermentation

Method

  1. Wash and soak rice and dal (fenugreek with dal) 4–6 hours.
  2. Grind dal fluffy, rice slightly coarse. Mix.
  3. Ferment warm 8–12 hours until risen.
  4. Add salt; thin for dosa or keep thick for idli.

Tips

  • Warm climate speeds fermentation.
  • Never add salt before fermenting.
