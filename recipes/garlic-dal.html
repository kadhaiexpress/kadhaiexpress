Garlic Dal

Comfort dal with fried garlic temper.

Ingredients

  • Toor or moong dal
  • Garlic
  • Cumin
  • Ghee
  • Salt

Method

  1. Cook dal soft.
  2. Fry garlic golden in ghee; pour over.

Tips

  • Do not burn garlic.
