Palak Paneer

Palak paneer combines blanched spinach and paneer in a gently spiced green gravy — a staple vegetarian curry across North India and beyond.

Ingredients

  • 250 g paneer, cubed
  • 500 g spinach, cleaned
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 tomato, chopped
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 green chillies
  • 1/2 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • 2 tbsp oil or ghee
  • Salt to taste
  • 2 tbsp cream or cashew paste (optional)

Method

  1. Blanch spinach in boiling water 2 minutes; cool in ice water. Blend with green chilli to a smooth puree.
  2. Heat oil; cumin; sauté onion until golden. Add ginger-garlic; then tomato; cook until soft.
  3. Add turmeric, coriander, salt. Stir in spinach puree; simmer 5–7 minutes.
  4. Add paneer cubes; cook 3–4 minutes. Finish with garam masala and optional cream.
  5. Serve with roti, naan, or jeera rice.

Tips

  • Do not over-boil spinach or the colour dulls.
  • Lightly pan-sear paneer before adding for better texture.
  • Cashew paste gives body without dairy cream.
