South Indian 25 mins 3 servings Vegetarian

Lemon Rice (Chitranna)

Lemon rice turns plain cooked rice into a bright, aromatic plate. Cool, separate grains and adding lemon off the heat keep the flavour fresh and the colour even.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups cooked basmati or sona masoori rice, cooled completely
  • 2 tablespoons oil
  • 1 teaspoon mustard seeds
  • 1 teaspoon urad dal
  • 1 teaspoon chana dal
  • 2 tablespoons raw peanuts
  • 2 dried red chillies
  • 1 sprig curry leaves
  • ½ teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 2–3 tablespoons fresh lemon juice (to taste)
  • Salt to taste
  • Optional: grated ginger, 1 green chilli, chopped coriander

Method

  1. Use rice that is fully cooled and fluffed so the grains are separate. Fresh hot rice clumps and turns mushy when mixed.
  2. Heat oil in a wide pan on medium heat. Add mustard seeds and wait until they pop. Add urad dal, chana dal, and peanuts; fry until the dals turn golden and the peanuts are crisp. Add dried red chillies, curry leaves, and optional ginger and green chilli. Sauté a few seconds.
  3. Lower the heat. Add turmeric and stir quickly so it colours the oil without burning.
  4. Add the cooled rice and salt. Toss gently on low heat for 2–3 minutes until every grain is coated and warmed through.
  5. Switch off the heat. Sprinkle lemon juice and mix. Taste and adjust salt and lemon. Garnish with coriander. Serve with coconut chutney, yogurt, or as a tiffin rice on its own.

Tips for best results

  • Add lemon only after switching off the heat for a fresher taste.
  • Day-old refrigerated rice works especially well.
  • Roasted cashews can replace or join the peanuts.
