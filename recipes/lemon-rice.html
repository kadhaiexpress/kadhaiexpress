Lemon Rice (Chitranna)

Lemon rice turns leftover or fresh cooked rice into a bright, tangy, aromatic plate. Peanuts, curry leaves, and turmeric make it a tiffin-box favourite across South India.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups cooked basmati or sona masoori rice, cooled
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp mustard seeds
  • 1 tsp urad dal
  • 1 tsp chana dal
  • 2 tbsp peanuts
  • 2 dried red chillies
  • Curry leaves
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • 2–3 tbsp lemon juice (to taste)
  • Salt
  • Optional: grated ginger, green chilli, coriander

Method

  1. Fluff cooled rice so grains separate.
  2. Heat oil; mustard. Add urad, chana, peanuts; fry until golden. Add chilli, curry leaves, optional ginger and green chilli.
  3. Lower heat; add turmeric. Quickly add rice and salt; toss gently.
  4. Turn off heat; add lemon juice; mix. Adjust salt and lemon.
  5. Garnish with coriander. Serve with coconut chutney or plain yogurt.

Tips for best results

  • Use day-old rice for best texture.
  • Add lemon off heat to keep flavour fresh.
  • Roasted cashews can replace or join peanuts.
