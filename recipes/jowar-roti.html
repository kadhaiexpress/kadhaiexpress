Jowar Roti

Soft jowar flatbread technique.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups jowar flour
  • Warm water
  • Salt

Method

  1. Knead pliable dough.
  2. Pat and cook both sides.

Tips

  • Press gently while cooking.
