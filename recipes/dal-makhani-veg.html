Dal Makhani (Home Style)

Dal makhani is slow-cooked black urad with a little rajma, finished with butter and cream for depth.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup whole urad
  • 1/4 cup rajma
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic
  • Butter
  • Cream
  • Kasuri methi
  • Cumin
  • Salt
  • Kashmiri chilli

Method

  1. Soak overnight; pressure-cook until very soft.
  2. Sauté cumin, ginger-garlic, tomato.
  3. Add dal, simmer long with butter.
  4. Finish with cream and kasuri methi.

Tips

  • Long simmer creates restaurant texture.
  • Mash some dal against the pot for body.
