Jeera Rice (Cumin Rice)

Jeera rice is the quiet hero of Indian vegetarian plates: fragrant basmati, whole cumin, and clean flavour that lets dal and sabzi shine.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 cups basmati rice, rinsed and soaked 20 minutes
  • 2.5–3 cups water
  • 1 tbsp ghee or oil
  • 2 tsp cumin seeds
  • 2 green cardamom (optional)
  • 1 bay leaf (optional)
  • 1 tsp salt
  • Coriander or fried onions to finish (optional)

Method

  1. Drain soaked rice.
  2. Heat ghee; add cumin (and whole spices if using) until they crackle and smell nutty—do not burn.
  3. Add rice; sauté gently 1 minute to coat grains.
  4. Add water and salt. Bring to a boil, then cover and cook on low until water is absorbed (about 12–15 minutes).
  5. Rest 5 minutes off heat. Fluff with a fork and serve.

Tips

  • Soaking basmati helps long, separate grains.
  • Ratio of water depends on rice age; start with 1:1.75 by volume after soaking.
  • Pair with dal tadka or any gravy sabzi.
