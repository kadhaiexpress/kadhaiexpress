Jeera Rice (Cumin Rice)

Jeera rice should have separate, fragrant grains. Soaking basmati, toasting cumin in ghee, and resting after cooking make the difference.

Ingredients

  • 1½ cups basmati rice
  • 2 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 tbsp ghee or oil
  • 2 green cardamom (optional)
  • 1 bay leaf (optional)
  • About 2½ cups water (adjust to rice)
  • 1 tsp salt

Method

  1. Rinse basmati until water runs clearer. Soak 20 minutes, then drain fully. Soaking helps long grains cook evenly.
  2. Heat ghee in a heavy pot. Add cumin seeds (and optional whole spices). Fry on medium heat until cumin turns a shade darker and smells nutty — do not burn.
  3. Add drained rice. Stir gently for 1 minute so each grain is coated in flavoured fat.
  4. Add water and salt. Bring to a boil, then cover and cook on the lowest heat until water is absorbed, about 12–15 minutes. Avoid stirring midway.
  5. Switch off the heat and rest, covered, 5 minutes. Fluff with a fork and serve with dal or any gravy sabzi.

Tips for best results

  • Water ratio varies with rice age; start with roughly 1:1.75 after soaking.
  • Resting finishes the grain without mush.
  • Ghee gives better aroma than neutral oil.
