Aloo Paratha

Aloo paratha stuffs soft whole-wheat dough with spiced mashed potato — a complete vegetarian breakfast with butter and curd.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups atta
  • 3 boiled potatoes
  • 1 tsp cumin powder
  • Chilli, amchur, garam masala
  • Salt
  • Oil/ghee
  • Coriander

Method

  1. Knead soft dough; rest.
  2. Mash potato with spices.
  3. Stuff, seal, roll carefully.
  4. Cook on tawa with ghee until golden both sides.

Tips

  • Cool filling reduces tearing.
  • Even rolling prevents leaks.
