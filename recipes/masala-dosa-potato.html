Masala Dosa Potato Filling (Aloo Masala)

The heart of a masala dosa is this soft, tangy-spiced potato filling. Master the aloo masala and any good dosa or even plain chapati becomes a feast.

Ingredients

  • 4 medium potatoes, boiled and roughly mashed
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 1 tsp mustard seeds
  • 1 tsp urad dal
  • 1 tsp chana dal
  • 10–12 curry leaves
  • 1–2 green chillies
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Salt to taste
  • Optional: grated ginger, lemon, coriander

Method

  1. Heat oil; mustard until it pops. Add urad and chana dal; fry until golden.
  2. Add curry leaves, chilli, onion; sauté until onion softens.
  3. Add turmeric and potatoes; salt; mix without crushing to paste. Sprinkle water if dry.
  4. Finish with coriander and lemon. Use inside dosas or as a side.

Tips

  • Slightly chunky potatoes taste better than fully mashed.
  • Hing is optional but traditional in many homes.
