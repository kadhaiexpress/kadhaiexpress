North Indian 1 hr 10 mins 4 servings Vegetarian

Malai Kofta (Vegetarian)

Malai kofta is a celebration dish: soft paneer-potato koftas in a silky cashew-tomato gravy. Bind the koftas well, fry a test piece, and add them to the gravy only at serving time so they stay intact.

Ingredients

  • Kofta: 1 cup grated paneer, 2 medium potatoes boiled and mashed, 2–3 tablespoons cornflour or besan, salt, red chilli powder, pinch of garam masala, oil for frying
  • Gravy: 2 onions roughly chopped, 2 tomatoes roughly chopped, 12–15 cashews soaked, 1 tablespoon ginger-garlic paste, ½ teaspoon turmeric, 1 teaspoon coriander powder, ½ teaspoon garam masala, 2–3 tablespoons cream or milk, 2 tablespoons oil or ghee, salt, coriander to garnish

Method

  1. For the koftas, mix grated paneer, mashed potato, cornflour or besan, salt, chilli powder, and garam masala into a soft dough that holds shape. If it feels sticky, add a little more binder. Shape into smooth balls.
  2. Heat oil for frying. Fry one test kofta first — if it breaks, mix a bit more cornflour into the remaining dough. Fry the rest on medium heat until evenly golden. Drain on paper and set aside.
  3. For the gravy, sauté onion until soft. Add tomato and cook until mushy. Cool slightly, then blend with soaked cashews into a very smooth paste. Add a splash of water if needed for blending.
  4. In a clean pan, heat oil or ghee. Add ginger-garlic paste; stir briefly. Pour in the blended paste. Cook on medium heat, stirring, until the mixture thickens and the oil begins to separate. Add turmeric, coriander powder, and salt. Cook another minute.
  5. Add water or milk to reach a flowing gravy consistency. Simmer 5 minutes. Stir in cream and garam masala. Taste and adjust salt and mild sweetness if the tomatoes are sharp.
  6. Just before serving, place koftas in a serving bowl and ladle hot gravy over them (or simmer koftas in gravy for only 1–2 minutes). Garnish with cream and coriander. Serve with naan or pulao.

Tips for best results

  • Never boil koftas for long in the gravy or they disintegrate.
  • A smooth cashew paste is what gives restaurant body.
  • Bake or air-fry koftas for a lighter version; texture will be slightly different.
