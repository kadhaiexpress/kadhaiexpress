Malai Kofta (Vegetarian)

Malai kofta is a celebration vegetarian curry: soft paneer-potato koftas in a luxurious cashew-tomato gravy. Best for special meals with naan or pulao.

Ingredients

  • Kofta: 1 cup grated paneer, 2 boiled potatoes mashed, 2 tbsp cornflour or besan, salt, chilli, garam masala
  • Gravy: 2 onions, 2 tomatoes, 12 cashews, ginger-garlic, 1/2 tsp turmeric, 1 tsp coriander powder, 1/2 tsp garam masala, cream or milk, oil/ghee, salt

Method

  1. Mix kofta ingredients; form balls. Deep-fry or air-fry/bake until golden. Set aside.
  2. Make gravy: sauté onion; blend with tomato and soaked cashews. Cook paste with spices until oil separates.
  3. Add water/milk; simmer. Finish with cream and garam masala.
  4. Add koftas just before serving so they stay intact. Garnish with cream and coriander.

Tips

  • Fry a test kofta — if it breaks, add a little more binder.
  • Do not boil koftas long in gravy.
  • Cashew paste is the body of restaurant-style gravy.
