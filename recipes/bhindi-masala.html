Bhindi Masala (Spiced Okra)

Bhindi masala rewards careful prep: fully dry okra, open-pan cooking at the start, and enough time for the stickiness to cook off before onions and tomatoes go in.

Ingredients

  • 250–300 g bhindi (okra), washed, completely dried, cut into 1-inch pieces
  • 1 medium onion, sliced
  • 1 medium tomato, chopped
  • 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
  • ½ teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 teaspoon coriander powder
  • ½ teaspoon red chilli powder
  • ½ teaspoon amchur (dry mango powder) or lemon juice
  • 2 tablespoons oil
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh coriander, optional

Method

  1. Wash the bhindi and spread it on a clean cloth or kitchen towel until every piece is fully dry. Moisture is the main cause of sliminess. Trim the tops and cut into even 1-inch pieces.
  2. Heat oil in a wide kadhai on medium-high heat. Add cumin seeds and let them crackle. Add the bhindi in a single layer as much as possible. Sauté, stirring often, for 6–8 minutes. You will see the surface colour change and the sticky strands reduce. Do not cover the pan in this stage.
  3. When the okra looks less sticky and lightly sautéed, add the sliced onion. Cook until the onion softens and turns translucent.
  4. Add the tomato, turmeric, coriander powder, chilli powder, and salt. Mix gently. Cook uncovered until the tomato softens and any extra moisture evaporates. The sabzi should look coated, not stewy.
  5. Sprinkle amchur or a squeeze of lemon, toss once, and garnish with coriander if using. Serve with roti or as a side with dal-rice.

Tips for best results

  • Never cover okra at the start of cooking.
  • A wide pan helps moisture escape faster than a deep pot.
  • Amchur brightens the finished dish without adding water.
