Rava Upma

Upma is a savoury semolina breakfast tempered with mustard, dal, curry leaves, and often vegetables. Texture should be soft but not pasty.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup rava (sooji), dry roasted
  • 2.5–3 cups water
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • Optional: carrot, peas, beans
  • 1 tsp mustard
  • 1 tsp urad dal
  • Curry leaves, green chilli, ginger
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Salt, lemon, coriander

Method

  1. Roast rava until aromatic; set aside.
  2. Heat oil; mustard, urad, chilli, curry leaves, ginger. Sauté onion and vegetables.
  3. Add water and salt; bring to a boil.
  4. Lower heat; slowly add rava while stirring to avoid lumps. Cover 2–3 minutes.
  5. Fluff, add lemon and coriander. Serve with coconut chutney or banana.

Tips

  • Roasting rava prevents raw taste and gumminess.
  • Water ratio controls softness — start with 2.5:1.
  • Ghee on top is optional richness.
