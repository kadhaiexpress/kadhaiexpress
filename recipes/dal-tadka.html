Dal Tadka (Restaurant-Style Yellow Dal)

Dal tadka is comfort in a bowl: soft yellow lentils finished with a sizzling tempering of ghee, cumin, garlic, and red chilli. This version aims for the creamy, aromatic dal served with rice or roti at home.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup toor dal (arhar), rinsed
  • 3 cups water (plus more as needed)
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp salt, or to taste
  • 1 tbsp ghee or oil
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 4–5 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 1–2 dried red chillies
  • 1/4 tsp hing (asafoetida), optional
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 1 tomato, chopped
  • 1–2 green chillies, slit
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • 1/2 tsp red chilli powder (optional)
  • Fresh coriander leaves
  • Lemon wedge to serve

Method

  1. Pressure-cook dal with water, turmeric, and salt until soft and mashable (about 3–4 whistles), or simmer in a pot 35–45 minutes.
  2. Whisk or lightly mash dal to a creamy consistency; add hot water if too thick. Keep warm.
  3. Heat ghee in a small pan. Add cumin, dried red chilli, and garlic; fry until garlic is golden. Add hing if using.
  4. In a kadhai, sauté onion until golden. Add tomato and green chilli; cook until soft. Stir in coriander and chilli powder.
  5. Pour dal into the masala. Simmer 5–8 minutes. Adjust salt and consistency.
  6. Pour the hot garlic-cumin tadka over the dal. Garnish with coriander. Serve with rice or roti and lemon.

Tips

  • A final tadka in ghee makes the aroma restaurant-like.
  • For richer dal, mash a portion completely and leave some texture.
  • Vegan option: use oil instead of ghee.
