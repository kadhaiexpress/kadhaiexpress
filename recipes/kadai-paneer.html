Kadai Paneer

Kadai paneer features paneer and capsicum in a tomato-forward gravy with coarsely ground coriander-chilli masala.

Ingredients

  • 250g paneer
  • 1 capsicum
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 onion
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic
  • 1 tbsp coriander seeds
  • 2 dry red chillies
  • Kasuri methi
  • Oil
  • Salt

Method

  1. Dry-roast coriander and chilli; crush coarsely.
  2. Sauté onion, ginger-garlic, tomato.
  3. Add kadai masala, salt, capsicum; then paneer.
  4. Finish with kasuri methi.

Tips

  • Coarse masala is the signature texture.
  • Keep capsicum slightly crisp.
