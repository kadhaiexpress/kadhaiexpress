Matar Paneer

Matar paneer is a festive-yet-everyday curry: soft paneer and sweet green peas in a balanced masala gravy. It sits comfortably beside roti, naan, or pulao.

Ingredients

  • 200 g paneer, cubed
  • 1 cup green peas (fresh or frozen)
  • 2 onions, chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, pureed
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 tbsp oil or ghee
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • 1/2 tsp red chilli powder
  • Salt, coriander, optional cream

Method

  1. Heat oil; cumin. Sauté onion until golden. Add ginger-garlic; then tomato; cook until oil separates.
  2. Add turmeric, chilli, coriander; salt. Add peas and a splash of water; cook until peas are tender.
  3. Add paneer; simmer 4–5 minutes. Finish with garam masala and optional cream.
  4. Garnish with coriander. Serve hot with roti or jeera rice.

Tips for best results

  • Lightly fry paneer before adding for better texture.
  • Frozen peas work well — no need to thaw fully.
  • Cashew paste can enrich the gravy without cream.
