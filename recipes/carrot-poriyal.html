Carrot Poriyal

Carrot poriyal is quick, colourful, and coconut-finished.

Ingredients

  • 3 carrots grated or diced
  • Mustard
  • Curry leaves
  • Coconut
  • Green chilli
  • Oil
  • Salt

Method

  1. Temper spices.
  2. Add carrot and salt.
  3. Finish with coconut.

Tips

  • Grated carrot cooks faster.
