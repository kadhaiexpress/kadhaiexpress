Rajma Masala (Kidney Bean Curry)

Rajma masala is the soul of many North Indian vegetarian tables: creamy red kidney beans in a deep, spiced tomato-onion gravy. Paired with plain rice, it is comfort food at its best.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 cups dried red kidney beans, soaked 8–12 hours (or 2 cans, rinsed)
  • 2 large onions, finely chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, pureed
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 tbsp oil or ghee
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp red chilli powder (adjust)
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp kitchen king or rajma masala (optional)
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh coriander, ginger juliennes

Method

  1. Pressure-cook soaked rajma with salt and enough water until very soft (several whistles). Reserve cooking liquid. Mash a few beans for body.
  2. Heat oil; cumin. Sauté onions on medium-low until deep golden — this is the flavour base.
  3. Add ginger-garlic; cook until raw smell goes. Add tomato puree; cook until oil separates.
  4. Add powdered spices; stir. Add rajma with enough liquid to make a thick gravy.
  5. Simmer 15–20 minutes so beans absorb the masala. Finish with garam masala and coriander.
  6. Serve hot with steamed rice, onion salad, and lemon.

Tips for best results

  • Undercooked rajma is unpleasant — beans must mash easily.
  • Deep golden onions make restaurant-style colour and sweetness.
  • Canned beans: simmer longer in the masala to improve texture.
