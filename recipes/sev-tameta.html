Sev Tameta (Tomato Sev Curry)

Sev tameta is a quick Gujarati curry: soft tomatoes in a mildly sweet-sour gravy finished with crunchy sev. Ideal with rotli or khichdi.

Ingredients

  • 4 ripe tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 tsp mustard seeds
  • 1/2 tsp cumin
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp red chilli powder
  • 1 tsp coriander-cumin powder
  • 1 tsp sugar or jaggery
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Salt
  • 1 cup nylon sev
  • Coriander

Method

  1. Heat oil; mustard and cumin. Sauté onion until soft.
  2. Add tomato, turmeric, chilli, coriander-cumin, salt, sugar. Cook until tomatoes break down into a thick gravy.
  3. Adjust water for a flowing but not watery consistency.
  4. Turn off heat; top generously with sev and coriander just before serving.

Tips

  • Add sev only at the end so it stays crisp.
  • Slight sweetness balances tomato acidity.
