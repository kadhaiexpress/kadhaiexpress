Gobi Paratha

Gobi paratha uses grated spiced cauliflower as filling inside whole-wheat flatbread.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups atta
  • 2 cups grated cauliflower
  • Ajwain, chilli, garam masala
  • Salt
  • Oil
  • Ginger optional

Method

  1. Squeeze excess moisture from grated gobi.
  2. Mix with spices.
  3. Stuff into dough balls; roll; cook on tawa with oil.

Tips

  • Dry filling is critical.
  • Cook on medium heat for even browning.
