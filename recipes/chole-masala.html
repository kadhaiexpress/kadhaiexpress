Chole Masala (Chickpea Curry)

Chole should be soft, well-spiced, and slightly tangy. A deep-fried onion colour and enough simmer time in the masala give the gravy body without needing shortcuts.

Ingredients

  • 1½ cups dried chickpeas, soaked overnight (or 2 cans, rinsed)
  • 2 large onions, finely chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, pureed
  • 1 tablespoon ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 tablespoons oil
  • 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
  • 1 teaspoon coriander powder
  • 1 teaspoon chole masala or garam masala
  • ½ teaspoon red chilli powder
  • ½ teaspoon turmeric
  • 1 teaspoon amchur (dry mango) or 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • Optional: 1 black tea bag while boiling chickpeas for colour
  • Salt, coriander leaves, ginger juliennes

Method

  1. Soak chickpeas 8–12 hours. Drain. Pressure-cook with water, salt, and optional tea bag until very soft. Discard tea bag. Reserve cooking liquid.
  2. Heat oil. Add cumin, then onions. Cook onions on medium heat until deep brown — this step builds the signature dark gravy colour. Add ginger-garlic paste; cook until fragrant.
  3. Add tomato puree and cook until the oil separates. Add turmeric, chilli, coriander powder, and salt. Stir well.
  4. Add chickpeas and enough cooking liquid. Simmer 15–20 minutes. Mash a few chickpeas to thicken. Finish with chole masala and amchur or lemon.
  5. Garnish with coriander and ginger. Serve with bhature, puri, rice, or roti.

Tips for best results

  • Soft chickpeas are non-negotiable.
  • Amchur at the end brightens the gravy.
  • Canned chickpeas work if simmered longer in masala.
