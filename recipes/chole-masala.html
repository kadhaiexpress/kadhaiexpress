Chole Masala (Chickpea Curry)

Chole is a bold chickpea curry — central to Punjabi-style vegetarian plates, stuffed kulchas, and simple rice meals.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 cups dried chickpeas, soaked overnight (or 2 cans, rinsed)
  • 2 onions, finely chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, pureed
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • 1 tsp chole masala or garam masala
  • 1/2 tsp red chilli powder
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp amchur or 1 tbsp lemon
  • Tea bag or 1 tsp tea leaves in pouch for colour (optional)
  • Salt, coriander, ginger juliennes

Method

  1. Cook soaked chickpeas with salt (and optional tea) until very soft. Reserve some cooking liquid.
  2. Heat oil; cumin; onions to deep golden. Add ginger-garlic; tomato; cook until oil separates.
  3. Add powdered spices; then chickpeas and liquid. Simmer 15–20 minutes, mashing a few chickpeas for body.
  4. Finish with amchur/lemon and garam masala. Garnish and serve with bhature, rice, or roti.

Tips

  • Deep-fried onion colour builds restaurant-style depth.
  • Canned chickpeas work on busy days—simmer longer in masala.
