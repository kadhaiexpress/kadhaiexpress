Vegetable Biryani (Dum Style)

Vegetable biryani is built in layers: part-cooked fragrant rice, spiced vegetables, herbs, and a sealed dum so steam finishes the grains without turning them mushy.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups aged basmati rice, soaked 30 minutes
  • 2 cups mixed vegetables (carrot, beans, peas, potato, cauliflower)
  • 2 onions, thinly sliced (half for gravy, half fried for topping)
  • ½ cup yogurt
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • Whole spices: 1 bay leaf, 4 cloves, 4 cardamom, 1 small cinnamon stick
  • 1 tsp biryani masala or garam masala
  • ½ tsp turmeric
  • Saffron pinch soaked in 3 tbsp warm milk
  • Mint and coriander leaves
  • 3 tbsp ghee or oil
  • Salt

Method

  1. Rinse basmati until water runs clearer; soak 30 minutes, then drain. Bring a large pot of water to a rolling boil with salt and half the whole spices. Add rice and cook only until about 70% done — the grain should still have a firm white core. Drain immediately.
  2. Heat ghee/oil in a heavy pot. Fry a portion of sliced onions until deep golden; remove for garnish. In the remaining fat, add rest of whole spices, then onion, then ginger-garlic. Add vegetables, turmeric, biryani masala, and salt. Sauté, then add yogurt and cook until vegetables are almost tender and the masala coats them.
  3. Level the vegetable layer. Spread the drained rice on top. Sprinkle fried onions, chopped mint and coriander, saffron milk, and a few drops of ghee.
  4. Seal the pot with a tight lid (or dough seal). Place on a low flame over a heavy tawa for 15–20 minutes dum. Alternatively finish in a 180°C oven for 20 minutes.
  5. Rest 10 minutes without opening. Open, fluff gently from the side, and serve with raita.

Tips for best results

  • Never fully cook rice before dum.
  • Fried onions add sweetness and aroma.
  • A tawa under the pot reduces burning on the base.
