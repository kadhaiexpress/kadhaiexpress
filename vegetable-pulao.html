Vegetable Pulao

Vegetable pulao is a complete one-pot meal when the rice is soaked, the vegetables are cut evenly, and the water ratio is controlled so grains stay separate.

Ingredients

  • 1½ cups basmati rice, rinsed and soaked 20 minutes
  • 2 cups mixed vegetables (carrot, beans, peas, potato), cut small
  • 1 onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 tablespoon ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 tablespoons oil or ghee
  • 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
  • 2 cloves, 1 bay leaf, 1 small cinnamon stick, 2 green cardamom
  • ½ teaspoon turmeric
  • 1 teaspoon salt, or to taste
  • About 2¾ cups water (adjust to your rice)
  • Coriander and lemon, to serve

Method

  1. Rinse basmati until the water runs clearer, then soak 20 minutes and drain fully. Soaking helps long grains cook evenly without breaking.
  2. Heat oil or ghee in a heavy pot on medium heat. Add cumin and the whole spices. When they crackle and smell fragrant, add the sliced onion and cook until light golden.
  3. Add ginger-garlic paste and stir for 30–40 seconds until the raw smell softens. Add the mixed vegetables and turmeric. Sauté 2–3 minutes so the vegetables pick up the spice oil.
  4. Add the drained rice and salt. Stir gently for about a minute so every grain is coated — do not stir hard or the rice will break.
  5. Pour in the water. Taste the liquid for salt. Bring to a boil, then cover and cook on the lowest heat until the water is absorbed and the rice is tender, about 12–15 minutes. Avoid opening the lid repeatedly.
  6. Switch off the heat and rest, covered, for 5 minutes. Fluff gently with a fork. Serve with raita, plain yogurt, or a simple dal.

Tips for best results

  • Cut vegetables small and even so they cook with the rice.
  • If the pulao is undercooked, sprinkle 2 tablespoons hot water, cover, and rest on low heat 3 more minutes.
  • Ghee gives a better aroma than neutral oil alone.
