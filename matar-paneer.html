North Indian 40 mins 4 servings Vegetarian

Matar Paneer

Matar paneer is a balanced curry of soft paneer and sweet green peas in a tomato-onion gravy. Building the masala slowly and adding paneer only at the end keeps the cubes soft.

Ingredients

  • 200 g paneer, cut into cubes
  • 1 cup green peas (fresh or frozen)
  • 2 medium onions, finely chopped
  • 2 medium tomatoes, pureed or finely chopped
  • 1 tablespoon ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 tablespoons oil or ghee
  • 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
  • ½ teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 teaspoon coriander powder
  • ½ teaspoon red chilli powder
  • ½ teaspoon garam masala
  • Salt to taste
  • 2 tablespoons cream or cashew paste (optional)
  • Fresh coriander, to garnish

Method

  1. If the paneer is fridge-hard, soak the cubes in warm water for 10 minutes, then drain. This softens the texture before it goes into the gravy.
  2. Heat oil or ghee in a kadhai on medium heat. Add cumin seeds and let them crackle. Add the onions and cook until they turn golden — pale onions leave a raw taste in the gravy.
  3. Add ginger-garlic paste and stir for 30–40 seconds until the raw smell goes. Add the tomato and cook until soft and the oil begins to separate from the masala.
  4. Add turmeric, coriander powder, red chilli powder, and salt. Stir for 20–30 seconds so the spices bloom in the oil.
  5. Add the green peas and a splash of water (about ¼–½ cup). Cover and cook until the peas are tender — fresh peas need a bit longer than frozen. The gravy should look thick but still spoonable; add a little more hot water if it dries out.
  6. Gently slide in the paneer cubes. Simmer on low heat for only 3–5 minutes so the paneer warms through and picks up flavour without turning rubbery. Stir in garam masala and optional cream or cashew paste. Taste for salt.
  7. Garnish with coriander and serve with roti, naan, or jeera rice.

Tips for best results

  • Add paneer late so it stays soft.
  • Cashew paste gives body if you prefer not to use cream.
  • Frozen peas work well; no need to thaw fully before cooking.
