Coconut Chutney

Coconut chutney is best when ground slightly coarse, salted correctly, and finished with a hot mustard-curry leaf tempering.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup grated fresh coconut (or thawed frozen)
  • 1–2 green chillies
  • Small piece of ginger (optional)
  • 2–3 tablespoons roasted chana dal (optional, for body)
  • Salt to taste
  • Water as needed
  • For tempering: 1 teaspoon oil, ½ teaspoon mustard seeds, ½ teaspoon urad dal, 1 dried red chilli, curry leaves, pinch of hing

Method

  1. Add coconut, green chilli, optional ginger, optional roasted chana dal, and salt to a mixer jar. Grind with small splashes of water to a paste that is smooth enough to spoon but still has a little texture. Too much water makes watery chutney.
  2. Taste and adjust salt and chilli. Transfer to a serving bowl.
  3. Heat oil in a small pan. Add mustard seeds; when they pop, add urad dal and fry until light golden. Add dried red chilli, curry leaves, and hing. Pour the full tempering over the chutney.
  4. Mix lightly or leave the tempering on top for aroma. Serve with idli, dosa, or vada. Best the same day.

Tips for best results

  • Fresh coconut tastes brightest; frozen works if fully thawed.
  • Roasted chana dal thickens and mildens sharp chilli heat.
  • Make small batches — refrigerated chutney loses freshness quickly.
