Mumbai style 50 mins 4 servings Vegetarian

Pav Bhaji

Pav bhaji is a buttery mashed vegetable mash spiced with pav bhaji masala and served with toasted pav. Soft vegetables and enough butter make the street-style texture at home.

Ingredients

  • 2 potatoes, peeled
  • 1 cup cauliflower florets
  • ½ cup green peas
  • 1 carrot, chopped
  • 2 large tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 large onion, finely chopped
  • 1 tablespoon ginger-garlic paste
  • 2–3 tablespoons pav bhaji masala
  • ½ teaspoon turmeric
  • Salt to taste
  • Butter, as needed (2–4 tablespoons)
  • Oil 1 tablespoon
  • Pav buns
  • Lemon, chopped onion, coriander, to serve

Method

  1. Boil potato, cauliflower, peas, and carrot with a little salt until very soft. Drain (keep a little cooking water) and mash coarsely — the bhaji should be textured, not baby food.
  2. Heat 1 tablespoon oil and 1 tablespoon butter in a wide pan. Add onion and cook until soft and light golden. Add ginger-garlic paste; stir until fragrant.
  3. Add tomatoes and cook until fully soft and mushy. Add turmeric, pav bhaji masala, and salt. Cook 2 minutes so the masala loses its raw smell.
  4. Add the mashed vegetables. Mix well, adding reserved water or hot water to reach a thick, spreadable mash. Simmer 8–10 minutes on medium heat, mashing further with a potato masher. Stir in more butter for richness.
  5. Taste and adjust salt and pav bhaji masala. The flavour should be bold because it is eaten with plain pav.
  6. Slit pav, toast on a hot tawa with butter until golden. Serve bhaji topped with butter, chopped onion, coriander, and lemon wedges.

Tips for best results

  • Pav bhaji masala is the main flavour — use a blend you like.
  • Mash while simmering to get the classic texture.
  • Extra butter is traditional; reduce if you prefer lighter.
